Keiran Sweet

Cloud & AI Platform Engineer

Cloud and AI Platform Engineer with over 20 years of experience spanning the full arc from physical Unix data centres to modern multi-cloud and AI-native platform environments. My focus and passion lies in building platforms that help developers, engineers, and technologists move fast with new technologies to rapidly deliver business value.

One of the first 10 employees at Sourced Group, I joined at inception and grew with the company through its expansion across Australia, Canada, and globally before its acquisition by Amdocs in 2021 — building teams, delivering at scale, and shaping how cloud infrastructure is designed and operated across Finance, Telco, Aviation, and Healthcare. Working across many companies and within Sourced, I have been fortunate to engage across a variety of verticals, giving me experience in a broad set of technologies, infrastructure, tools, and team structures.

I enjoy working in teams where I can share my knowledge and help team members grow their capabilities — whether through mentoring sessions, supporting podcasts, or presenting at conferences. I am based in the Southwest of Western Australia and work predominantly in a remote capacity.
